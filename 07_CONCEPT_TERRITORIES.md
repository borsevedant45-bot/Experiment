# 07 CONCEPT TERRITORIES

STATUS: LOCKED
DATE: 2026-10-04

## PURPOSE
Explore materially different creative worlds before selecting one.

### 01 — Living Kitchen
**Core metaphor:** kitchen as interface
**Visual world:** Illustrated kitchen re-composes around intent.
**Colour:** Parchment + brick + mustard + herb + cobalt.
**Typography:** Editorial display anchored to surfaces.
**Food representation:** Modular food SVGs.
**Motion:** Slide, snap, wobble, settle.
**Navigation:** Stations/counters.
**Hero:** Order assembles physically.
**Emotional feel:** Tactile, warm.
**Why different:** One world covers all stages.
**Risk:** Can get busy.
**Implementation difficulty:** Medium

### 02 — Food Orchestra
**Core metaphor:** order as choreography
**Visual world:** Ingredients behave like an ensemble.
**Colour:** Ink/cream + saturated “notes.”
**Typography:** Rhythmic display type.
**Food representation:** Ingredients as sections.
**Motion:** Sequenced entrances and beats.
**Navigation:** Kitchen stations.
**Hero:** A meal performs an assembly sequence.
**Emotional feel:** Energetic.
**Why different:** Motion itself becomes identity.
**Risk:** Can overperform.
**Implementation difficulty:** High

## SELECTION
**Primary: Living Kitchen.** It is the strongest single world because discovery, menu, cart, checkout and tracking can all be expressed as changes inside one environment.

**Secondary mechanics allowed:** choreography from Food Orchestra; fold/seal behaviors from Wrapper World. These are borrowed mechanics, not additional visual worlds.

## REJECTED PRIMARY DIRECTIONS
Night Market (too dense), Comic Delivery (tone risk), Taste Map (transactional fit risk), Delivery Theatre (too stage-like for utility).

## LOCKED DECISIONS
Living Kitchen is the creative north star. Do not average all 16 concepts together.
