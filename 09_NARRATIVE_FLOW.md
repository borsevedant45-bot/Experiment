# 09 NARRATIVE FLOW

STATUS: LOCKED
DATE: 2026-10-04

## PURPOSE
Treat the product journey as one continuous story.

| Stage | Goal | Emotion | Visual state | Interaction | Motion | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | orient | curious | quiet kitchen | explore | objects wake subtly | discoverability |
| Discovery | find desire | curious | craving field | search/category | ingredients cluster | relevant choices |
| Exploration | compare | playful | restaurant counters | select | counter responds | clear favorite |
| Restaurant | inspect | desirous | kitchen counter/menu | scroll/select | menu layers settle | dish confidence |
| Menu | build | decisive | plate/tray | add/quantity | food assembles | tangible feedback |
| Cart | verify | confident | packed collection | edit | re-pack | clear total |
| Checkout | complete | calm | packing station | address/payment | minimal motion | confidence |
| Confirmation | know | delighted | ticket/package | inspect | print/seal | memorable success |
| Tracking | wait | anticipatory | journey surface | inspect | route/stage updates | progress |
| Delivery | finish | satisfied | arrival state | done/reorder | settle | completion |
| Reorder | repeat | familiar | archive/tray | re-add | prior pack reconstructs | speed |

## PACING
**Accelerate:** discovery selection, quantity changes, reorder.
**Slow:** dish inspection, confirmation, tracking.
**Surprise:** first add, confirmation, arrival.
**Simplify:** checkout, payment, error recovery.
**Explode:** hero assembly and confirmation only.
**Calm:** immediately after high-energy transitions.

## TRANSITION RULE
At least one visual object should survive between major states so the user experiences continuity instead of a page cut.

## SOUND
Optional, off by default. Sound may reinforce object contact or confirmation but never carries necessary information.

## REJECTED DIRECTIONS
- Full-screen animation on every route.
- Tracking as decorative-only animation.
- State changes with no inherited object.

## LOCKED DECISIONS
The order journey is the narrative spine.
