# 02 EXPERIENCE BRIEF

STATUS: LOCKED
DATE: 2026-10-04

## PURPOSE
Define the emotional product experience before designing interface surfaces.

## EXPERIENCE PROMISE
> **Every order feels like a tiny food story: discover it, assemble it, send it, and watch it arrive.**

## EMOTIONAL TARGET
`curious → playful → desirous → decisive → anticipatory → rewarded`

Primary feelings: appetite + curiosity.
Peak: anticipation.
Exit: satisfaction + a small feeling of delight.

## USER PERCEPTION
> “This feels alive, but I always know what to do.”

Not:
> “This is a normal delivery app with effects added.”

## PERSONALITY
Mischievous editorial food world: warm, confident, tactile, slightly eccentric, never childish.

## PREMIUM DEFINITION
Premium means a defensible point of view, coherent illustration/motion, excellent micro-details and strong pacing—not maximum visual density.

## DIFFERENTIATION
The product is recognized by its behavioral signature: food and the order itself physically change state as users discover, add, pack, confirm and track.

## REJECTED DIRECTIONS
- Serious fine-dining luxury.
- Arcade-game hyperactivity.
- Cartoon mascot as the main brand.
- Cinematic spectacle that slows ordering.

## LOCKED DECISIONS
- The order journey is the emotional spine.
- Food and physical order objects carry personality.
- Fast/critical moments become visually quieter.

## DEPENDENCIES
03, 07, 09, 10, 11, 12, 13.

## OUTPUT
A usable emotional target and differentiation thesis.
