# 04 INSPIRATION UNIVERSE

STATUS: LOCKED
DATE: 2026-10-04

## PURPOSE
Research adjacent creative disciplines to extract mechanics and principles—not surfaces to copy.

## RESEARCH UNIVERSE
### Motion design studios
Ordinary Folk publishes a process that moves from message/story to look-and-feel, then animation, while considering audio from the start. The lesson for this project is causal sequencing: story and visual language first, animation craft second, audio as an integrated optional layer. Source: https://www.ordinaryfolk.co/process

### Editorial design
Useful mechanics: hierarchy through asymmetry, pacing, large/small image relationships, annotation and controlled negative space.

### Packaging
Useful mechanics: fold, label, seal, reveal, collect, carry and handoff.

### Food illustration
Useful mechanics: appetite through silhouette, color, ingredient grouping and tactile object behavior rather than realism.

### Kinetic typography
Useful mechanics: type as spatial marker, state signal and rhythm—not as permanently moving decoration.

### Physical menus/signage
Useful mechanics: environmental labels, stations, tickets, counter-top organization and memorable typography.

### Animation/cartoon timing
Useful mechanics: overshoot, settle, anticipation, cause-and-effect chains and expressive pauses.

### Interactive storytelling
Useful mechanics: environment changes, inherited objects between states and one or two high-salience moments.

## EXTRACTED PRINCIPLES
1. Story before surface.
2. Look-and-feel determines motion.
3. Repeating objects create identity.
4. Physical metaphors make state transitions tangible.
5. Contrast between spectacle and calm creates pacing.
6. Sound is a complement, never a dependency.

## REJECTED DIRECTIONS
- Direct competitor-screen imitation.
- “Awwwards clone” aesthetics.
- Trend collage without a product metaphor.
- Visual references used as final compositions.

## LOCKED DECISIONS
The project draws from editorial, packaging, animation and food illustration as mechanics libraries.
