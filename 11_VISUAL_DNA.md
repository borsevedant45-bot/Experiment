## TYPOGRAPHY
**Display:** expressive serif or humanist display family with personality. **OPEN** until existing repository fonts/licensing are verified.

**Body:** neutral sans with strong legibility. **OPEN** until inventory is verified.

**Numbers/labels:** same system, stronger weight, tight tracking.

**Kinetic type:** only display/section labels; body copy stays stable.

Suggested scale: hero 64–112px desktop / 42–64px mobile; section 32–56px; card 18–26px; body 14–18px.

## ILLUSTRATION
Flat 2D vector, slightly imperfect curves, controlled charcoal outlines, opaque graphic fills, modular internal parts, flat offset shadows, simple front/three-quarter perspective.

## SHAPES
Irregular rectangles, trays, plates, labels, tickets and soft circles/ovals. Avoid default 16px rounded-card treatment as the primary visual motif.

## COMPOSITION
Editorial asymmetry, negative space, overlap, one anchor object per major viewport, strong large/small relationships, restrained repetition.

## REJECTED DIRECTIONS
Glassmorphism, generic gradients, glossy 3D food, hyper-real texture, stock vector bundles.

## LOCKED DECISIONS
Visual identity = hand-drawn editorial food illustration + quiet paper environment + bold controlled food accents + physical object containers.
